Common questions

Where is my place saved?

Your place in each book is saved automatically as you read, on your device. Open the book again, or tap Continue reading in the Library, to pick up where you left off.

How does the reading streak work?

Read five pages in a day, in any book or in Discover, and your streak grows by one. Miss a day and it starts again.

I’m not getting the daily reminder.

Check that Remind me to read is on in Reading Settings (the gear in the Library), and that notifications are allowed for Scroll The Classics in your device’s Settings. The reminder is skipped on days you’ve already read your five pages.

How do I change the text size or turn on dark mode?

Tap the gear in the Library, or while reading, to open Reading Settings. Changes apply straight away.

Does the app need an internet connection or an account?

No. All books are included in the app, and nothing you do is sent anywhere. See the Privacy Policy.
