Scroll The Classics

Privacy Policy

Effective November 23, 2026

Scroll The Classics does not collect any personal data. Everything you do in the app stays on your device.

What the app stores, and where

To remember your reading, the app saves a few things only on your iPhone or iPad:

  • your place in each book and how far you have read
  • the passages you save to Favorites
  • your reading streak and today’s page count
  • your reading settings (text size, font, line spacing, appearance)
  • whether the daily reminder is on, and its time

This information is never sent to us or to anyone else. Like other app data, it may be included in your device backup (for example, an iCloud or computer backup), which is handled by Apple under Apple’s own privacy policy. Deleting the app deletes this information from your device.

What we don’t do

  • No accounts or sign-up are required.
  • No advertising, analytics or tracking of any kind.
  • No data is sold or shared with third parties.
  • The app does not connect to the internet. All books are included in the app.

Daily reminder

The optional daily reminder is a notification scheduled on your device itself. The app asks for permission only when you turn the reminder on, and you can turn it off at any time in the app or in your device’s Settings.

Sharing

When you choose to share a passage, it goes through your device’s standard share sheet to the app or person you pick. Scroll The Classics does not see or keep what you share.

Children

Because the app collects no personal data from anyone, it collects none from children.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app changes how data is handled, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date above will change.
